Joel Coen and Ethan Coen · 2009

  • How does God speak to us?

How does God speak to us?

Larry wants to know what message he's supposed to be receiving:

Larry
Is Hashem trying to tell me that Sy Ableman is me? Or that we are "all one," or something?

But the rabbi is more interested in how the message is delivered:

Rabbi
How does God speak to us? A good question!

This wasn't Larry's question, but the rabbi doesn't care. He's just excited to tell Larry about his favorite story, as evidenced by his gleefulness, which starkly contrasts with Larry's distress. (Larry's lawyer later asks, "What, did he tell you about the goy's teeth?") The rabbi then brings up Lee Sussman and asks Larry:

Rabbi
Did he ever tell you about the goy's teeth?
Larry
No. What goy?

It's so pitiful. Larry is not on the same wavelength as the rabbi at all. The rabbi hardly mentions the goy in his story, and at the end, Larry asks again:

Larry
And... what happened to the goy?
Rabbi
The goy? Who cares?
